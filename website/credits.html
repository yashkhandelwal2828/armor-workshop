Credits & privacy

Armor Workshop is an unofficial Iron Man fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Marvel. Iron Man, Tony Stark, and associated characters and marks belong to their respective owners.

Source models

  • Iron Man Mark 7 — listing credit: Gource; embedded download credit: Baconsoup. Both credits are retained because the source records differ.
  • Iron Man Mark 85 — uploaded by LLIypuk; modeling credit: Harlow fx.

Source records identify both models as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Adaptations include mesh repair, extraction, remeshing, proportion changes, material edits, modular armor, and thumbnail renders. Model creators do not endorse this project.

Mark VII and Mark 85 derivatives appear in the suit, helmets, inventory components, and preview images. Workshop geometry, service robots, interface, and synthesized mechanical audio were created for this project. Comic and film references informed the designs; reference images are not distributed in the website.

Local settings

The application stores your armor, paint, and render quality in your browser. No accounts, analytics, microphone access, or server-side profile storage are included. Reset restores the default suit; clearing this site's browser data removes saved settings. Your hosting provider may keep ordinary request logs.

About the simulation

Assembly and free roam are interactive visual simulations. Armor names and mechanisms describe fan concepts, not functioning engineering or exact canonical replicas.
